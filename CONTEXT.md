# Domain Context: Alchemist Nayon

## 1. System Overview
**Alchemist Nayon** is an elite chemistry coaching academy located in Arabpur, Jessore (opposite SP Bungalow Gate), directed and mentored by **Mohamed Nayon** (*"Experiment is my passion 🔬, Teaching is my mission 🎯"*). 

The platform serves secondary and tertiary students across Jessore and surrounding regions:
- **HSC Chemistry Care**: Complete 1st and 2nd paper curriculum mastery for HSC 2027 and HSC 2028 batches, combining NCTB board-book fundamentals with medical, engineering, and varsity admission preparation.
- **Honours Chemistry Care**: Advanced undergraduate chemistry guidance for National University and degree college students.

## 2. Ubiquitous Language & Core Concepts

- **Mentor (নয়ন স্যার)**: Mohamed Nayon, founder and head teacher, framed as an accessible, passionate mentor who demystifies complex chemistry through real-world experiments and interactive digital boards.
- **Batch Program (ব্যাচ প্রোগ্রাম)**: Distinct academic groupings categorized by exam year (HSC 2027, HSC 2028, Honours).
- **Batch Routine (সময়সূচি)**: Fixed, non-shifting weekly time slots categorized by day-group (e.g. রবি-মঙ্গল-বৃহস্পতি, শনি-সোম-বুধ) and time of day (সকাল, দুপুর, বিকাল, সন্ধ্যা, রাত).
- **Alchemist Laboratory (স্মার্ট ক্লাসরুম)**: The interactive digital smart board and physical lab classroom where 3D molecular structures and reactions are visualized.
- **Hall of Fame (সাফল্যের ইতিহাস)**: Verified past academic achievements, board exam GPA-5.00 results, and top medical/engineering/university admission admissions.
- **Primary Conversion Action**: Direct phone call (`017...`) and physical campus visit to the Arabpur Jessore center (opposite SP Bungalow Gate) with clear landmark directions (no digital map embeds). Online booking facilitates pre-registration.

## 3. Visual Identity & Interface Architecture

Per `DESIGN.md`, the interface follows the **Cohere 2026 Enterprise AI Web System**:
- **Pure Light Mode**: No dark mode support; clean, high-contrast, distraction-free daylight readability.
- **Canvas & Surface**: Pure canvas white (`#ffffff`), warm soft stone (`#eeece7`), and deep enterprise green/navy contrast bands (`#003c33`, `#071829`).
- **Typography**: Editorial display headlines with tight tracking (`Space Grotesk` / `Inter`) paired with clean Bengali typography (`Hind Siliguri`) and monospaced chemistry telemetry (`JetBrains Mono` / `ui-monospace`).
- **Layout Model**: Asymmetric Bento grids replacing standard repetitive vertical card stacks.
- **Components & Radius**: Pill-shaped primary actions (`rounded-full`), 22px/16px media cards, crisp 1px hairline dividers (`#d9d9dd`), and coral category chips (`#ff7759`).
